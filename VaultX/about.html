About Our Project

Encrypted Credentials Manager

In today's digital world, managing multiple usernames and passwords for different websites and applications can be difficult.

Encrypted Credentials Manager is a secure application designed to store and manage login credentials in one organized and protected place.

The system uses a Master Password to protect the user's vault. After successful authentication, users can add, view, search, update, and delete their credentials securely.

Our goal is to provide a simple, secure, and user-friendly way to manage sensitive login information and prevent unauthorized access.
